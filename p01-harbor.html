Questions

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Call (240) 747-7755 or message us on WhatsApp.

How do online lessons work?

Every lesson is live and one-on-one with an experienced teacher, from home. Lessons are adapted to the student's level, goals and pace.

How long is each lesson, and how often?

Choose 30, 45 or 60-minute lessons. Most families start with one lesson a week; students can take 2–5 lessons a week.

How much does it cost?

The standard 4-lesson plan is $110 for 30-minute lessons, $150 for 45 minutes and $200 for 60 minutes.

Do you support neurodiverse learners?

Yes. Because every lesson is one-on-one, teachers adjust the pace and approach to how each student learns best.

Can my career training be funded?

If you live in New Jersey, workforce and vocational rehabilitation programs may cover your training. Contact us and we'll help you check.

Ready to meet your teacher?

Tell us who's learning and what you'd like to achieve. Our Cranbury, New Jersey team will match you with the right teacher.
